2 · August · the diagnosis

The lift did not die. It got diluted 3 to 1.

Inside the flat average are two very different groups of students.

26% chose the lab
67% · 11.4%
task submission · P1 completion

Above control on both. These are July's high-intent students. The signal is still there.

74% walked past it
53% · 8.4%
task submission · P1 completion

Below control. An offer they did not ask for, dropped at the payment moment, bought nothing.

What the experiment reports
flat
the blend of both

One part lift, three parts drag. Averages to zero. High-intent users got washed in with everyone else.

One honest caveat: students chose which group to be in, so this describes who Labs works for, not yet what Labs causes. The next experiment is designed to answer that cleanly.
Takeaway: the signal is diluted, not gone.4 / 7
3 · The next play

Labs First: make every trialer high-intent from minute one

Combine July's audience with August's traffic. Same lab we have today, one new placement.

  • 1Retire the doors. The gated experiment closes with its honest verdict, no detectable effect at this sample, and the door comes off the payment page.
  • 2Open every trial with the lab. Matched to the vertical the student picked at registration, so intent is built in, not hoped for. One visible skip, no nagging.
  • 3Traffic becomes a non-issue. 100% of trialers see it instead of 26%. Clean 50/50 test at trial start, kill switch, 2-3 tickets, fully reversible.
The big swing: manufacture the first win, then ask for Project 1.5 / 7

3 · Why this moves Project 1

Both experiments point the same way. Lab-first is sized to prove it on P1.

Every high-intent read points the same direction and none is proven. We're not claiming it works. We're claiming the signal is consistent enough, and the cost low enough, that the right move is the test that settles it.

(a) High intent moves P1
+8 pts · +5 pts
week-1 work, high-intent students vs control: July test · August choosers

Task 1 is the proxy that pulls P1. On P1 itself, August choosers ran 11.4% vs 8.7% control, the right direction but only 8 of 70 students, which is why we size a real test instead of calling it.

(b) Traffic makes it count
26% → 100%
share of trialers who experience the lab

Matched to their registered vertical, so intent is built in, not hoped for. ~160-175 students per arm per week: enough volume to read fast and to move the company number.

How we will know
Sept 30
powered task read · P1 mid-Oct · ship wk of Aug 25

Pre-registered floors, one decision date, kill conditions written before launch. 2-3 tickets, reversible by flag.

Honest footnote: the 11.4% comes from students who chose the lab, so it is a propensity signal, not causal proof. Lab-first is the randomized test that turns it into one.
