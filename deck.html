2 · Why flat

High-intent users got washed in with everyone else

The 26% who chose to enter
67% · 11.4%

Task 1 and Project 1 rates. Above control on both. These are July's high-intent seekers, still in there.

The 74% who walked past
52.5% · 8.4%

Below control. An offer they did not ask for, at the worst moment, bought nothing.

The blend the board sees
flat

26% above + 74% below averages out to control. The signal is not gone, it is diluted.

Honest footnote: the 26/74 split is self-selected, so it describes who uses Labs, not what Labs causes. That is exactly the question the next experiment is designed to answer properly.
The product works when chosen. Delivery washed the signal out.4 / 7
3 · The next play

Labs First: combine both. Intent, at full traffic.

  • Kill the gated doors experiment. Its honest verdict: no detectable effect either way at the achievable sample. The paywall door comes off the payment page entirely.
  • The trial opens with the lab. Same lab as today, moved to the front: matched to the vertical each student picked at registration, so everyone arrives high-intent by construction. Visible skip.
  • Traffic becomes a non-issue. 100% of trialers see it instead of 26%. Clean 50/50 test at trial start, kill switch, 2-3 tickets, fully reversible.
